Questions

How long does it take?

48 hours after we receive your completed questionnaire or house manual.

What do you need from me?

The general area, check-in method, Wi-Fi, house rules, appliance notes, checkout steps, emergency contacts and your favorite local spots. If you already have a house manual, that’s usually enough.

Do my guests need to install an app?

No. The guidebook is a simple web page that opens on any phone from a link or a QR code.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay once. Changes after delivery are $15 per update, only if you need them.

Which languages do you offer?

English, French, Spanish and Arabic. Other languages on request.

Do you recommend places you haven’t checked?

No. Local recommendations come from you. Anything we suggest is clearly marked for your approval before publishing.

I already use Touch Stay or Hostfully.

Great — we can write and translate the content for your existing guidebook. That’s the part the software doesn’t do for you.

What if I’m not happy?

We revise until it’s right within your included revision rounds. If you’re still not satisfied, we refund 100% before you publish the guidebook.
